# v0.1.0-beta scope and playtest checklist

| Area | Included | Playtest on the integrated build |
|---|---|---|
| Gadgetron and PDA | Original-source shop and ammo-only PDA; private sessions, Left/Right browsing, quantity repeat, explicit confirmation, and atomic purchases | Check unfolding, ticker and portrait across navigation, both funded and unfunded purchases, inventory caps, and two shoppers at once. The earlier live `STALE_OFFER` report and Blaster showcase angle were not confirmed. |
| Blaster and Bomb Glove | Dedicated ammunition; purchasable Blaster and one-time free Bomb Glove claim with ten bombs; ammo refills and crate rewards; original-source models, equip cues, wrist fit, and dry-burst effects | Check both configured hands for the Glove, thrower and team protection, remote presentation, and terrain preservation. Red RaC explosive-crate chaining is not implemented in this candidate. |
| Clank, Gear and Hydro-Pack | Globally single-claim natural wrecks, physical Back slot and gifting, decorative pod recovery, Heli and Thruster movement, and submerged Sprint toggle with W-release braking | Race two players for a rescue, restart and retest both claim and escrow, check full-inventory return, and verify underwater controls, bubbles and audio. PAL scripted Hydro audio is not supported. |
| Bolt Grabber and Survival supplies | One active Belt slot, equipped bolt attraction, worn and pickup art, natural crate and vendor placement, and additive chest loot for the PDA and Bolt Grabber | Check remote Belt rendering, exact arrival credit, newly generated structures, and unopened versus already-filled chests across reload. |
| OmniWrench and bolt ledger | Iron crafting recipe, updated action sound and grip scale, return-only wrench attraction, and operator `/bolts` grants to self or targets | Check crafting, action timing and sound, target selection, and non-operator denial. |
| Green Travel Ship | Persistent single-seat Overworld flight, Creative placement and empty-ship removal, side access, hull collision, and Turbo at 30 forward / 12 up / 10 down blocks per second | Check parked and occupied cockpit, both sides of boarding and exit, collision, stop and restart, Turbo and camera. The +X captain's chair remains unresolved; the pilot stays centered. The occupied opening and engine audio are Minecraft adaptations. Survival acquisition is not implemented. |

Box Breaker, a second working Belt slot, purchasable Clank upgrades, ship combat, passengers, planet travel and natural Travel Ship spawning are outside this candidate. Original assets are read from a verified player-local disc image and are not distributed with the mod.
